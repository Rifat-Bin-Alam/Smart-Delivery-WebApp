Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

SwiftDrop collects necessary contact information (name, email address, mobile phone number) and delivery address details required to process, fulfill, and deliver your orders accurately.

2. Usage of Your Data

We use your information exclusively to dispatch assigned couriers, provide SMS/email order tracking updates, calculate appropriate delivery fees, and improve platform service offerings. We never sell customer records to third-party advertisers.

3. Courier Access

Assigned delivery partners are provided your delivery location and contact number solely during an active delivery task to ensure successful handoff.

4. Data Security

We employ industry-standard encryption, secure session tokens, and strict access controls to safeguard your personal credentials and order history.
